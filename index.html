About

I received my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2026, advised by Prof. Gongshen Liu and Assoc. Prof. Zhuosheng Zhang. In September 2026, I will join the CTIC Lab at the National University of Singapore (NUS) as a Postdoctoral Researcher, advised by Prof. Mong-Li Lee and Prof. Wynne Hsu.

My research interests include MLLM Agent, Geospatial Reasoning, World Modeling, and AI Scientist.

📣 Prospective collaborators: I am always open to research collaborations. If you are interested in working with me, please feel free to reach out by email—especially if you are based in Singapore. 欢迎邮件联系我讨论合作，尤其欢迎在新加坡的同学。
